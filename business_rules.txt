# BUSINESS RULES – ONLINE FOOD DELIVERY SYSTEM

This file contains the business rules used by the Online Food Delivery
Knowledge Repository.

The rules are applied to the facts stored in the knowledge base to
generate useful business decisions.

---

## RULE 1: LOW FOOD AVAILABILITY

Condition:
If the availability of a food item is less than 10.

Decision:
Low Availability Alert

Example:
If the availability of Paneer Biryani is 5, the system generates:

Decision: Low Availability Alert

---

## RULE 2: PREMIUM CUSTOMER

Condition:
If the customer is a Premium member AND the order amount is greater
than ₹500.

Decision:
Free Delivery

Example:
If a Premium customer places an order worth ₹600, the system generates:

Decision: Free Delivery

---

## RULE 3: FAILED PAYMENT

Condition:
If the payment status of an order is Failed.

Decision:
Payment Pending

Example:
If the payment for an order fails, the system generates:

Decision: Payment Pending

---

## RULE 4: DELIVERED ORDER

Condition:
If the order status is Delivered.

Decision:
Order Successfully Delivered

Example:
If an order has been delivered successfully, the system generates:

Decision: Order Successfully Delivered

---

## RULE 5: HIGH-VALUE ORDER

Condition:
If the order amount is greater than ₹1000.

Decision:
Priority Order

Example:
If a customer places an order worth ₹1500, the system generates:

Decision: Priority Order

---

## RULE 6: HIGHLY RATED RESTAURANT

Condition:
If the restaurant rating is greater than or equal to 4.5.

Decision:
Recommended Restaurant

Example:
If the rating of Spice Hub is 4.5, the system generates:

Decision: Recommended Restaurant

---

## RULE 7: LARGE QUANTITY ORDER

Condition:
If the quantity of food items in an order is greater than or equal
to 5.

Decision:
Large Order Alert

Example:
If a customer orders 5 or more food items, the system generates:

Decision: Large Order Alert

---

## SUMMARY OF BUSINESS RULES

1. Low Food Availability
   IF food availability < 10
   THEN Low Availability Alert

2. Premium Customer
   IF membership = Premium
   AND order amount > ₹500
   THEN Free Delivery

3. Failed Payment
   IF payment = Failed
   THEN Payment Pending

4. Delivered Order
   IF order status = Delivered
   THEN Order Successfully Delivered

5. High-Value Order
   IF order amount > ₹1000
   THEN Priority Order

6. Highly Rated Restaurant
   IF restaurant rating >= 4.5
   THEN Recommended Restaurant

7. Large Quantity Order
   IF quantity >= 5
   THEN Large Order Alert

---

## PURPOSE

These seven business rules allow the knowledge repository to process
stored facts and generate decisions for an online food delivery
business system.

The rules demonstrate rule-based decision making using knowledge
representation and show how a real-world business system can use
stored knowledge to generate automated decisions.
